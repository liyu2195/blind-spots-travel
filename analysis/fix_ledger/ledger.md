# Claim ledger: every claim the project tested

Numbers are copied from the outputs named in each line (paths relative to `analysis/`); the only new computations are in `fix_ledger/`
(L01 collapse inventory, L02 cost table without collapsed runs, L03 seed counts, L04 leniency curve without collapsed runs, L05 cost account of
the remaining grader families). The ledger was written on 16 November 2026, before the last analyses of the paper (the own vote as the grader,
the transitions, forced decoding; those are listed in `INDEX.md`) and before the outputs were written again on the final set of runs. Where a
number here differs from the output file it names, the output file is the later one.

**Legend.** Q = Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (discovery split); L = Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (confirmation split); S = small students (phi4m,
smol3, granite; out-of-sample check). "Pooled" = both main students weighted equally. Shares are 0-1 unless "points" or "per 100" is
given; [a, b] = 95% interval of the analysis named.
- **Verdict**: PASS = holds in Q and L under the stated test; MIXED = holds pooled or in one student only, or only with a caveat that
  changes the wording; FAIL = the test fails or the effect is absent or reversed; INCONCL. = underpowered.
- **Chosen**: PH = pattern found while looking at the data, tested afterwards; PH* = post hoc claim, but the estimand and test were
  written down before the number was computed (stats/, verify_*/, dose/ helpers: "conventions stated before any estimate was computed");
  PRE = criterion or prediction fixed before the outcome was computed on data not used to find it; REV = check added after an internal review of a draft.

**How many patterns were examined.** The first round of exploration reported 22 (dynamics) + 25 (answers) + 22 (knowledge) + 20 (meta) checked
patterns plus an unrecorded number for judges: at least 89, of which at least 10 failed or held only as an ordering .
Rounds 1-6 left 318 analysis scripts and 343 text outputs in 36 folders. This ledger has 95 claims in sections A-F (43 PASS,
33 MIXED, 10 FAIL, 1 INCONCL., 8 findings, magnitudes or disclosures without a pass/fail test) and 14 pre-specified or forward tests
in section G (7 met, 4 failed, 3 mixed).

**Holm summary of the ten primary tests** (`stats/s99_summary.py` -> `stats/s99_summary_out.txt`): all ten pass pooled (largest
adjusted p 0.013). Per student, four fail: on Llama (confirmation) C1 content stays home (adj. p 0.077) and C7 copy beats scramble
(0.057); on Qwen (discovery) C3 lenient judge = noise (0.12) and C8 visible beats hidden (0.10). Of the two stated "no effect"
secondaries, C2b no step effect fails on Qwen (0.14) and C8b hidden set carries none fails pooled (0.29).

## H. Disclosure paragraph (ready to paste into the appendix)

Most analyses were chosen after looking at the data; we discovered on Qwen and confirmed on Llama, and report every test we ran
(109 claims and tests; 318 analysis scripts). All ten primary tests pass Holm correction when the two students are pooled (largest
adjusted p = 0.013). Applied to each student separately, four fail: on Llama, "the judge-specific paid content stays on the training
questions" (equivalence p = 0.077; bound 0.056) and "a copy of a judge's rejections transfers more than a scrambled copy" (adjusted
p = 0.057); on Qwen, "a lenient judge is indistinguishable from random noise on new questions" (equivalence p = 0.12; Qwen -1.9 points
[-3.8, -0.2] and Llama +1.2 [+0.0, +2.5], so the pooled equivalence averages opposite effects) and "rejections planted on visible
relation types transfer more than on a hidden half" (adjusted p = 0.10). Measured against key-trained runs, the judge-specific paid
content on new questions is 9% [3, 16] of its size on training questions: small, but not zero. On Qwen the paid share of wrong training
answers also rises slightly with training steps at a fixed visit number (+0.026 per 100 steps [+0.007, +0.044]; equivalence within
+-0.04 fails, p = 0.071). A hidden half of each relation type carries little, but equivalence to zero fails (+0.031 [-0.034, +0.115];
p = 0.29). The leniency curve does not hold outside the training recipe (R^2 -0.22 over 105 runs that change the "I don't know"
credit, the KL weight, the advantage estimator or the run length) nor on the Granite student (R^2 0.04). A drift model with no fitted
constants predicted the wrong sign for runs with one forced "I don't know" per group (Qwen: predicted -1.9 points, observed +9.3 and
+6.8). Two forward tests of "predictable rejections transfer further" failed: the ranking within TriviaQA (rank correlation +0.31
[-0.18, +0.72]) and the Granite judge, the least predictable judge, which shows the largest effect.
