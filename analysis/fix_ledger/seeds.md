## What this means for the paper

- The **independent units** for the judge-specific claims are configurations, not runs: 26 student x dataset x judge units and 44 judge
  pairs at the strict cut, 5 distinct judges (4 on TriviaQA and PopQA). The Qwen judge has 6 seeds per dataset; every other judge has
  1-3, and the Granite judge exists only on NQ-open with 2 seeds.
- **Single-seed configurations** after removing collapsed runs: Qwen with OLMo at the strict cut on TriviaQA; Mistral, Gemma and OLMo at
  P(Yes) > 0.5 on TriviaQA (both students); most out-of-recipe runs; most small-student runs.
- **One-dataset or one-grader claims**: revisits vs steps (TriviaQA, one judge), causal copies and planted sets (PopQA), capitals
  (PopQA-trained), false-accept mirror (NQ), strictness dose-response (Qwen judge only).
- Every interval in the cost account (magnitudes.md M1) resamples questions only; the location and content claims use crossed
  (question x run) bootstraps or run-label permutations.
- Suggested caption convention: "n runs (seeds per configuration), m configurations; intervals resample questions and runs".
