Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): the answers and logs of the
runs, the judges' scores, the audit labels, the labels of the human check, the capitals probe, the fresh questions, the tables and figures.

The question sets in `data/` are samples of existing datasets and remain under the licences of those datasets:

- NQ-open (files `nqo_*`): CC BY-SA 3.0.
- TriviaQA (files `tq*`) and PopQA (files `pq*` except the capitals probe `pqq_*` and the fresh questions `pqx_*`, which we wrote): see the original releases.

Model outputs were generated with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Phi-4-mini, SmolLM3, Granite, and the judges named in the paper;
the terms of those models apply to the models, not to this repository.
