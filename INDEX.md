# Where each number in the paper comes from

Paths are relative to `analysis/` unless they start with `paper/`, `runs/`, `data/` or `training/`. Folder names are working names.
Raw outputs: `runs/run1/rl/<run>/eval_rl_ck120.jsonl` (answers after training), `runs/run1/adeval/probe2/<run>.jsonl` (capitals probe),
`runs/run1/adeval/fresh/<run>.jsonl` (fresh questions), `runs/run1/base/` (untrained samples and judge scores), `data/` (question sets; capitals probe
`pqq_1500.jsonl`; own-vote keys `<set>_h1_frzVOTE<k>_<student>.jsonl`), audit verdicts `analysis/extra/apitest/` (lookup `supp_v11/aud.py`).
Caches used by the newer scripts: `stats/cache/base_<student>_<set>.pkl`, `runs_<student>_<set>.pkl` (built by `stats/s01_cache.py` from the raw files).
